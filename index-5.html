How it's made

From grill to your hands.

  1. 1

    Marinate

    Paneer, mushroom and chicken are marinated in tandoori, chilli, butter garlic and Mexican spice blends.

  2. 2

    Grill & roll

    Fillings are grilled to order and rolled with fresh vegetables, or sealed and toasted into a UFO.

  3. 3

    Fry twice

    Hand-cut potatoes are fried in two stages so they stay crisp to the last one.

  4. 4

    Serve

    Handed over hot, with thick shakes, mojitos and desserts on the side.

Is everything made to order?

Yes. Every wrap is grilled when you order it, and every portion of fryz is fried fresh.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes: Classic Veggie, Butter Garlic Mushroom, Crispy Paneer and Cheesy Paneer wraps, plus all our fryz.

What is a UFO?

A UFO is a sealed, toasted round wrap with a hot filling inside. See the current flavours in our order app.

Can I order online?

Yes. Our full menu and prices are on the order app.
