08 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

And a few infrequently asked ones.

Is everything really made to order?

Yes. Every wrap is grilled and every fry is fried when you order. We have a strict no-sad-pre-made-wraps policy.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Absolutely: Classic Veggie, Butter Garlic Mushroom, Crispy Paneer and Cheesy Paneer, plus all our fryz.

What exactly is a UFO?

An Unidentified Flavour Object. A sealed, toasted wrap. Further details are classified (and in the order app).

Can I schedule a meeting with the Chief Crunch Officer?

His calendar is fully booked with being delicious. Please route all requests through the order app.
