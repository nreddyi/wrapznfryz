The craft

Marinate

Paneer, mushroom and chicken rest in spice blends before they ever see heat — tandoori, chilli, butter garlic, Mexican.

Grill

Fillings are grilled to order, then rolled with crisp vegetables into a warm wrap or sealed and toasted into a UFO.

Fry, twice

Potatoes are hand-cut and fried in two stages — once to cook through, once to crisp — then salted, served plain or loaded.

Serve

Handed over hot, alongside thick shakes, mint mojitos and desserts blended and plated right next to the fryer.
